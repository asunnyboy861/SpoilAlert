Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 22, 2026

1. Information We Collect

SpoilAlert is designed with privacy in mind. We collect minimal data:

  • Product Data: Skincare product names, brands, opening dates, and expiry information you enter. This data is stored locally on your device using SwiftData.
  • Photos: Product photos you take for PAO recognition are processed entirely on-device using Apple's Vision framework. Photos are never uploaded to any server.
  • Skin Journal Data: Your daily skin condition entries are stored locally on your device.
  • iCloud Data: If you subscribe to the Pro tier, your data syncs via iCloud using CloudKit with end-to-end encryption. We cannot access this data.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to provide the app's functionality:

  • Track product expiry dates and send reminders
  • Recognize PAO symbols using on-device OCR
  • Sync data across your devices (Pro tier only)

3. Data Storage

All data is stored locally on your device. Pro tier users' data is additionally synced via iCloud CloudKit, which is encrypted and managed by Apple. We do not maintain any external servers or databases.

4. Third-Party Services

SpoilAlert uses the following Apple services:

  • StoreKit: For in-app purchases and subscription management
  • Vision Framework: For on-device text and barcode recognition
  • CloudKit: For iCloud sync (Pro tier only)
  • UserNotifications: For expiry reminders

None of these services transmit your personal data to us.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal data with any third parties. Your data remains on your device and in your iCloud account.

6. Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as you use the app. You can delete all data by deleting the app from your device. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data.

7. Children's Privacy

SpoilAlert does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. The app does not require any personal information to use.

8. Your Rights

You have complete control over your data:

  • View all stored data within the app
  • Delete individual products or all data
  • Export your data (Pro tier)
  • Delete the app to remove all data

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date.
