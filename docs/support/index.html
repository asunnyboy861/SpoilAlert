FAQ

How do I add a product?

Tap the + button on the Dashboard or Products tab. Enter the product name, brand, category, opening date, and PAO period. You can also scan the PAO symbol with your camera.

What is a PAO symbol?

PAO stands for "Period After Opening." It's the small jar icon with a number and "M" (e.g., 12M) found on skincare packaging, indicating how many months the product is safe to use after opening.

How do I scan a PAO symbol?

When adding a product, tap "Scan PAO" to use your camera. Point it at the PAO icon on the product packaging. The app will recognize the number and automatically set the expiry period.

How do notifications work?

Plus and Pro subscribers receive reminders at 7 days, 3 days, 1 day, and on the expiry date. Free users receive a single reminder on the expiry date.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Go to Settings > Your Apple ID > Subscriptions, or tap "Manage Subscription" in the app's Settings tab.

Does SpoilAlert work offline?

Yes! All core features work offline. Only iCloud sync (Pro tier) requires an internet connection.
