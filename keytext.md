### Description (max 4000 chars)
SpoilAlert is your smart skincare expiry tracker that ensures you never use expired products again.

🧴 TRACK EVERY PRODUCT
Add your skincare products by name, brand, and category. Simply enter the opening date and PAO period, and SpoilAlert automatically calculates the exact expiration date.

📸 SCAN PAO SYMBOLS
Point your camera at the Period After Opening (PAO) icon on any product packaging. Our AI-powered recognition instantly reads the expiry period and sets the date for you.

🔔 SMART REMINDERS
Get timely notifications at 7 days, 3 days, 1 day, and on the expiry date. Never accidentally use an expired serum or moisturizer again.

✅ DAILY CHECK-IN
Track which products you use each day with a simple tap. Build a consistent skincare routine and see your usage patterns at a glance.

📓 SKIN JOURNAL (Plus)
Log your daily skin condition and discover which products work best for you. Track improvements over time with our visual skin trend chart.

☁️ iCLOUD SYNC (Pro)
Keep your product data synchronized across all your Apple devices. Start on your iPhone, continue on your iPad.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 FAMILY SHARING (Pro)
Share your SpoilAlert Pro subscription with up to 5 family members through Apple's Family Sharing.
